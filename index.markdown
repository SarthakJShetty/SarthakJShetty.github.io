I'm a Machine Learning Engineer at [Wayve](https://wayve.ai), working on pretraining. I was a Computer Vision Engineer at [Path Robotics](https://www.path-robotics.com), where I worked on end-to-end manipulation for [autonomous manufacturing robots](https://www.path-robotics.com/robotic-welding-systems/af1/) and large-scale vision systems for [autonomous welding robots](https://www.path-robotics.com/robotic-welding-systems/aw3/).

I graduated from [Carnegie Mellon University](https://cmu.edu) where I worked on [robot learning](https://sites.google.com/view/point-cloud-policy/home). I was advised by [Prof. Daniel Seita](https://danielseita.github.io/) and [Prof. David Held](https://davheld.github.io/) in the [Robots Perceiving and Doing Lab](https://r-pad.github.io/), which is a part of the [Robotics Institute](https://ri.cmu.edu). I worked on bimanual manipulation, grasping and using GANs for synthetic data generation.

I was a Research Intern at [Intel Labs](https://www.intel.in/content/www/in/en/research/overview.html), in the Silicon and Systems Prototyping Labs, where I worked on [SLAM](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/developer/tools/scenescape/overview.html). I worked on pose estimation, sparse map storage and retrieval, trajectory prediction and visual feature extraction.

Prior to grad school, I did research at the [Indian Institute of Science](https://iisc.ac.in) (IISc), working on [multi-robot](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/B9780128202760000236) [path-planning](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/9622590/), with [Prof. Debasish Ghose](https://aero.iisc.ac.in/people/debasish-ghose/) in the [Aerospace Engineering Department](https://aero.iisc.ac.in/).

I interned at the [Theoretical Ecology and Evolution Laboratory](https://teelabiisc.wordpress.com/) at IISc, advised by [Prof. Vishwesha Guttal](https://teelabiisc.wordpress.com/curriculum-vitae/), where I worked on [multi-robot systems](https://github.com/SarthakJShetty/Transient). I also interned at the [Center for Systems Design](https://csd.nitk.ac.in/) at the [National Institute of Technology - Surathkal](https://nitk.ac.in), advised by [Prof. KV Gangadharan](https://mech.nitk.ac.in/faculty/k-v-gangadharan), where I worked on imitation learning for [autonomous vehicles](https://github.com/SarthakJShetty/Donkey).

I'm deeply passionate about the outdoors and their conservation. I've worked with ecologists from [Columbia University](https://www.columbia.edu/), [Nature Conservation Foundation](https://www.ncf-india.org/) and [Panthera](https://panthera.org/). These projects have helped shape policy work around [wildlife monitoring in human conflict zones](https://conbio.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/conl.12815) and [analyzing threats to global biodiversity hotspots](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/ece3.8098): [[1](https://panthera.org/node/645)], [[2](https://newsroom.wcs.org/News-Releases/articleType/ArticleView/articleId/16262/Armed-Conflicts-a-Threat-to-Wildlife.aspx)], [[3](https://www.theswaddle.com/armed-conflicts-threaten-more-than-75-of-worlds-mammals-birds-study)], [[4](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/armed-conflicts-a-threat-to-wildlife-study/article34726899.ece)], [[5](https://www.africanelephantjournal.com/more-than-75-of-worlds-terrestrial-bird-mammal-species-witness-armed-conflict/)], [[6](https://science.thewire.in/environment/more-than-75-of-worlds-terrestrial-bird-mammal-species-witness-armed-conflict/)]

I am currently exploring robot manipulation using dense 3D features and scaling up (pre-)training for robot learning.
